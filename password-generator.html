About Secure Passwords

NetPeek's free password generator runs entirely in your browser using the Web Crypto API, producing truly random passwords that mix uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and symbols. Your passwords are generated locally on your device and never sent to a server, so you can create strong credentials with complete privacy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this password generator work?

NetPeek's password generator uses your browser's built-in cryptographic random number generator to create truly random passwords. Nothing is ever sent to a server, and none of your generated passwords are stored anywhere.

How long should my password be?

For maximum security, use a password of at least 16 characters that mixes uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. NetPeek generates strong 16-character passwords by default.

Is it safe to use an online password generator?

Yes, when it generates passwords entirely in your browser using a cryptographically secure random number generator, as NetPeek does. Your passwords are created locally on your device, never transmitted and never stored.

Should I use a password manager?

Yes. Once you generate a strong password, store it in a reputable password manager. This lets you use a unique, complex password for every account without needing to remember them all.
