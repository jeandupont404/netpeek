Privacy Policy

NetPeek · Last updated August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NetPeek ("we", "us", "our") — operated at netpeek.pp.ua — collects, uses and protects information when you visit and use our website and tools. NetPeek is a free service that helps you discover your public IP address, run network diagnostics and learn about the internet.

We are committed to being transparent and to complying with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where it applies to users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK.

1. Who is responsible for your data

NetPeek is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Because our site runs entirely in your browser and we do not operate our own servers, we do not store your personal data on our own infrastructure. However, several third-party services we use may process your data as described below. For any question about this policy, contact us at: privacy@netpeek.pp.ua

2. Data we process and why

NetPeek processes minimal data, and only in your browser. The table below lists the services involved and what they handle:

ServiceWhat it processesPurpose
api.ipify.orgYour public IP addressDisplaying your IP address on our tool
ipwho.isYour public IP addressProviding location, ISP, timezone and network details
dns.google (Google DoH)The domain name you type into the DNS toolRunning DNS lookups you request
Google AdSenseCookies and usage data for advertisingServing and measuring advertisements
Affiliate links (NordVPN, Surfshark, ExpressVPN)Click/referral data via affiliate networksMonetization; you are redirected to these providers

IP-based lookups

When you use the IP lookup tool, your browser sends your public IP address to api.ipify.org to retrieve it, and then to ipwho.is to obtain geolocation information. This processing happens directly between your browser and those third parties. We do not receive, store or log this information on our own servers.

DNS lookups

When you use the DNS Lookup tool, the domain name you enter is sent from your browser to Google's public DNS-over-HTTPS service (dns.google) to return the requested records. No domain is looked up without you explicitly submitting it.

3. Cookies and advertising

NetPeek itself does not set cookies. However, Google AdSense and Google's advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons and similar technologies to serve and personalize ads and to measure their performance. These cookies may be used to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant ads across the web.

Where required by law (in particular for users in the EEA and the UK), we obtain your consent before these advertising cookies are set. When you first visit our site, a consent banner asks you to accept or decline non-essential advertising cookies. Google AdSense is only loaded after you accept. Your choice is remembered on your device (in local storage), and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser data; you can also manage or disable cookies in your browser settings. Some legal bases under GDPR, such as "legitimate interests" for essential cookies, do not require consent.

You can also manage or disable cookies in your browser settings, and opt out of personalised advertising via Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info.

4. Affiliate links

Some links on our site (for example to VPN providers) are affiliate links. When you click an affiliate link, you may be redirected through an affiliate network that records the click for referral attribution. These networks may use cookies to determine that you arrived from NetPeek. Clicking an affiliate link is optional. See our Affiliate Disclosure for more details.

5. Data retention

NetPeek does not retain any personal data on its own servers. Any data handled by third-party services is subject to their own retention policies, which you can review on their respective privacy notices.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under the GDPR or other applicable law, including the right to:

  • Access the personal data processed about you;
  • Rectify inaccurate personal data;
  • Erase ("right to be forgotten") personal data, subject to legal limits;
  • Restrict or object to processing, including objecting to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • Data portability, where applicable;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal;
  • Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

Since most processing on our site is carried out by third parties, the most effective way to exercise these rights is by using the tools and settings provided by those third parties (for example, Google's Ads Settings and the consent banner). For anything else, contact us at privacy@netpeek.pp.ua and we will assist as best we can.

7. Children's privacy

NetPeek is a general-audience website and is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Where data is stored

When you use our site, your browser communicates directly with our third-party service providers. These providers may process data in and outside of your region, in accordance with their own data-transfer safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) where required by applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed of how we protect your information.

10. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at: privacy@netpeek.pp.ua
