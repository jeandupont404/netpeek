Affiliate Disclosure

NetPeek · Last updated August 28, 2026

Transparency matters to us. This page explains how NetPeek makes money from affiliate links, and how we keep our content honest and helpful regardless of any compensation we may receive.

What is an affiliate link?

Some of the links on this website — for example, links to VPN providers such as NordVPN, Surfshark and ExpressVPN — are affiliate links. This means that if you click on one of these links and then make a purchase or sign up for a service, NetPeek may earn a commission from the merchant, at no additional cost to you.

Does this affect our content?

No. Affiliate relationships do not change the content we publish. Our guides and reviews are written based on our own research and opinions. We only recommend products and services that we genuinely believe are useful and of good quality. Compensation does not influence what we recommend, nor does it affect the order in which products appear.

Which links are affiliate links?

Where a link may generate a commission for us through an affiliate or advertising relationship, we make reasonable efforts to label it as such (for example, with "sponsored" attributes or an inline disclosure) so you can make an informed choice. Clicking an affiliate link is always optional.

How affiliate links are tracked

When you click an affiliate link, you may be redirected through an affiliate network that uses a cookie or similar technology to attribute the referral back to NetPeek. This is standard practice across the web. It does not expose any additional personal information to us beyond what is described in our Privacy Policy.

Google AdSense & advertising

In addition to affiliate links, NetPeek may display advertising served by Google AdSense. Advertising revenue is separate from affiliate commissions and is also governed by our Privacy Policy and the consent choices you make on our site.
