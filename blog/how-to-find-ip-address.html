How to Find Your IP Address on Windows, Mac, iPhone and Android

NetPeek · Updated August 28, 2026

Every device connected to the internet has an IP address — a unique identifier that allows it to send and receive data. There are actually two you can look up: your public IP (the one the internet sees, assigned by your ISP) and your private IP (the one used only inside your local network). This guide shows you how to find both on any device.

Fastest way: Your public IP is the simplest to find. Just open our free IP address tool — it displays your public IP, location, ISP and timezone instantly, with no setup required.

How to find your public IP address

Your public IP address is the same for every device on your network, because it's assigned to your router by your internet service provider. The quickest way to see it is to visit a website that reports it back to you. Use our free IP lookup tool, or simply search "what is my IP" in any search engine.

How to find your IP address on Windows

Windows gives you a few different ways to find your IP address. The quickest is the command line.

Using Command Prompt (all Windows versions)

  1. Press Windows + R to open the Run dialog.
  2. Type cmd and press Enter to open Command Prompt.
  3. Type ipconfig and press Enter.
  4. Look for the IPv4 Address line in the active network adapter section — that's your private IP.

Using Windows 11 Settings

  1. Open Settings (Windows + I).
  2. Go to Network & internet → Wi-Fi (or Ethernet).
  3. Click on the connected network.
  4. Your IPv4 address is listed under the network properties.

Using Windows 10 Settings

  1. Open Settings and go to Network & Internet.
  2. Select Status.
  3. Click View your network properties.
  4. Scroll to find your IPv4 address.

How to find your IP address on macOS

  1. Click the Apple menu and open System Settings (or System Preferences on older versions).
  2. Go to Network.
  3. Select your active connection — Wi-Fi or Ethernet.
  4. Click Details next to the connected network.
  5. Your IP address is shown in the TCP/IP tab.

How to find your IP address on iPhone (iOS)

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap Wi-Fi.
  3. Tap the "i" icon next to your connected network.
  4. Your IP address appears in the IPv4 Address section.

How to find your IP address on Android

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Network & internet → Wi-Fi (exact wording varies by manufacturer).
  3. Tap the connected network.
  4. Tap Advanced or View more.
  5. Your IP address is shown in the network details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my IP address the same on my phone and computer?

Your public IP is the same for all devices on the same network, because they share the router's public connection. Your private IP is different on each device.

Why does my IP address keep changing?

Most home connections use dynamic IPs — your ISP periodically reassigns a new one, often after a router restart. That's normal and usually harmless.

Can someone find my exact location with my IP?

An IP reveals your approximate location (city or region) and your ISP, not your home address. It's not precise enough to locate your home, though a VPN adds extra privacy.
