Frequently Asked Questions

How do I test latency to a server?

Enter any URL, domain or IP address in the field above and click Test Latency. NetPeek sends a request to that server directly from your browser and measures the round-trip time (RTT) in milliseconds.

What is latency and round-trip time (RTT)?

Latency is the time it takes for data to travel from your device to a server and back, called the round-trip time (RTT). It's measured in milliseconds (ms) and is a key indicator of connection responsiveness, especially for gaming, video calls and live streaming.

What is a good latency (ping) number?

Generally, a ping under 20ms is excellent, 20-50ms is good, 50-100ms is average, and anything above 150ms can cause noticeable lag for gaming or video calls. Lower is always better.
